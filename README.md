# Power Sector Data Hub – Online Portals

A single page that links to the Indian power sector portals that are **working** and lets you open their data and reports. Every link opens the source portal in a new tab, and each one has a copy button.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build or install is needed.

## Portals listed (tested live on 19 Nov 2026)

| Area | Portal | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution | [RDSS](https://rdss.powermin.gov.in/dashboard) | AT&C loss, ACS–ARR gap, sanctioned/released funds, smart metering |
| Grid & generation | [National Power Portal (CEA)](https://npp.gov.in/publishedReports) | Daily/monthly generation, coal, capacity reports (PDF, Excel) |
| Grid & generation | [Grid-India daily PSP report](https://grid-india.in/en/reports/daily-psp-report) | Daily all-India power supply position (PDF, Excel) |
| Market & money | [PRAAPTI](https://praapti.in/) | Generator invoices and DISCOM overdue payments |
| Market & money | [IEX market data](https://www.iexindia.com/market-data/real-time-market/market-snapshot) | DAM, RTM and GDAM bids, volumes and clearing prices |
| Policy & research | [NITI Aayog ICED](https://iced.niti.gov.in/) | Energy and climate time series with Excel download |
| Policy & research | [Prayas Energy](https://energy.prayaspune.org/) | Renewable energy and transmission data portals |

## Not working at the last check (left out of the main list)

## Notes

- The headline figures on the page are a snapshot taken on 19 Nov 2026. They do not update by themselves.
- The direct Excel/PDF links to the Grid-India report point to the 17 Nov 2026 file. The report page link always has the newest file.
- Most government portals do not allow being shown inside another page, so the hub links to them instead of embedding them.
